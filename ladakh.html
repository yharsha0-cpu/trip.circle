Ladakh – Leh, Nubra, Turtuk & Pangong

6 Nights / 7 Days

Duration 6 Nights / 7 Days
Group Size 12–18 people
Stay 3/4 Star Hotels & Camps
Trip Type Adventure Group

About This Trip

Embark on a soul-stirring 6 Nights 7 Days journey through Ladakh, the land of high passes. Begin with acclimatization in the historic town of Leh before crossing the legendary Khardungla Pass into the stunning Nubra Valley. Ride double-humped Bactrian camels on the high-altitude sand dunes of Hunder, explore the border village of Turtuk, and marvel at the changing blues of the high-altitude Pangong Lake. Traverse the formidable Changla Pass and immerse yourself in ancient monasteries and rich cultural heritage. This group expedition is packed with jaw-dropping landscapes, mountain passes, and friendships that will last a lifetime.

Brief Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Leh
Arrive at Leh airport (3,500m) and transfer to your hotel. Acclimatization is key today. Rest completely in your hotel to let your body adjust to the high altitude. In the evening, visit the local Leh Market or Shanti Stupa for panoramic views of the city.
Day 2 – Leh Local Sightseeing
Explore Leh's rich culture. Visit the historic Leh Palace, Hall of Fame, Magnetic Hill, and Sangam (confluence of Indus and Zanskar rivers). Stop by the beautiful Gurudwara Pathar Sahib and Spituk Monastery.
Day 3 – Leh to Nubra Valley
Drive to Nubra Valley via the legendary Khardungla Pass (one of the world's highest motorable roads at 17,582 ft). Descend into Nubra Valley, check into your camp/hotel, and visit the giant Maitreya Buddha statue in Diskit. Enjoy a double-humped Bactrian camel ride on the Hunder Sand Dunes.
Day 4 – Turtuk Excursion
Take a day trip to Turtuk, a village right next to the Line of Control (LoC) that was under Pakistani control until 1971. Explore the unique Balti culture, apricot orchards, and historic Balti heritage museum. Return to Nubra Valley in the evening.
Day 5 – Nubra to Pangong Lake
Drive from Nubra to the stunning blue Pangong Lake via the Shyok river route. The lake, situated at 14,270 ft, spans across the India-China border. Watch the lake change colors from azure to turquoise under the setting sun. Spend the night at a lakeside camp.
Day 6 – Pangong to Leh
Wake up early to witness a breathtaking sunrise over Pangong Lake. Drive back to Leh crossing the high-altitude Changla Pass (17,688 ft). En route, visit Hemis or Thiksey Monastery, one of the largest and most picturesque monasteries in Ladakh. Check back into your hotel in Leh for a farewell dinner.
Day 7 – Departure from Leh
Bid goodbye to the land of high passes. Transfer to Leh airport for your flight back home with memories of towering mountains, azure lakes, and warm Ladakhi hospitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What flights should I book?
Book your flights arriving at Leh airport (IXL) on the morning of Day 1. Most flights to Leh land in the morning due to wind conditions. Our team will arrange your airport pickup.
Are permits required?
Yes, Inner Line Permits (ILP) are required for domestic and international travelers to visit areas like Nubra Valley, Turtuk, and Pangong Lake. These permits are included in your package price and will be pre-arranged by our team.
Who can join this trip?
Anyone in good physical health can join! Acclimatization is key, and we have planned the itinerary to allow slow ascending. However, please consult your physician if you have pre-existing cardiovascular or respiratory conditions.
